Cipher Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Cipher ("the App") helps you practice coding interview problems and track your progress. This policy explains what data the App handles, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account

  • Cipher creates an anonymous account automatically so your progress is saved. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number to use the App.
  • The account is identified by a random ID generated by our backend provider, Supabase.
  • Optional: Sign in with Apple. If you choose to back up your progress with Sign in with Apple, your account is linked to your Apple ID so your progress can be restored if you reinstall the App or get a new device. Apple shares with us a unique identifier and, depending on your choices in the Apple sign-in sheet, your name and an email address (which may be Apple's private relay address). We use these only to operate your account, not for marketing.

1.2 Your Progress and Submissions

  • Your XP, streak, and practice history.
  • The code you write and submit, the programming language, the result (for example pass or fail), and run time.

1.3 Optional Profile Details

  • If you choose a display name, it is stored with your account.
  • A profile photo you pick is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers.

1.4 Purchases

If you subscribe to Cipher Pro, Apple processes the payment. The App learns whether your subscription is active through Apple's StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment details.

1.5 What We Do Not Collect

  • No advertising identifiers and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos library access, or microphone/camera access.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Save and display your progress, streaks, and history.
  • Run your code and show you the results.
  • Generate AI Coach feedback on your solutions.
  • Provide Cipher Pro features while your subscription is active.
  • Keep the service secure and prevent abuse (for example, limiting how many AI Coach requests one account can make per day).

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who We Share It With

We use a small number of service providers to operate the App:

  • Supabase stores your account (anonymous, or linked to Apple if you use Sign in with Apple), progress, and submissions.
  • Judge0 (hosted through RapidAPI) receives the code you run so it can be executed and the output returned. Run requests are sent through our own server, which does not include your name, email, or account ID in the request to Judge0.
  • OpenAI receives your code and the problem text when you use AI Coach, so it can generate feedback. Requests are sent through our own server. Your name and account ID are not included in the request to OpenAI.
  • Apple processes subscriptions and purchases, and provides Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

These providers process data only to deliver their service to us. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Storage and Security

  • Data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS.
  • Preferences such as theme and difficulty are stored on your device.
  • We keep your account data until you delete your account.

5. Your Choices and Rights

5.1 Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time in the App: Settings → Delete Account. This removes your progress and submissions from our servers and erases the data stored on your device.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Cipher Pro subscription. To cancel, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

5.2 Clear Local Data

You can clear cached data in the App under Settings → Clear Cache.

5.3 Export Your Data

You can export your progress from Settings → Export Data.

5.4 Other Requests

For any other privacy request, including access or correction, contact us at the address below.

6. Children's Privacy

Cipher is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available in the App under Settings → Privacy Policy, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
